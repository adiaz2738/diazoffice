Selling a home is a big decision anywhere. Selling one in Monterey County comes with a few extra things to know, like city inspection reports, water rules on the Peninsula, fire zone paperwork, and micro-markets where two homes a few blocks apart can sell for very different prices.

This guide walks you through all of it, even if you've never sold a home before.

## Who this guide is for

I wrote it for the sellers I work with most often:

- **Homeowners selling for the first time,** or for the first time in a long time.
- **Families selling an inherited home or trust property,** often from out of the area.
- **Second-home owners** selling a vacation home or rental here.
- **People selling and buying at the same time,** whether they're moving across town or out of the area.

If you already know how selling works in California, you can skip straight to the local chapters. If you're new to all of it, start at the beginning.

## A few things to keep in mind

**Rules change.** Local reports, water rules, fire zone requirements, and tax rules all change over time. Each chapter shows when it was last checked, and I update them as things change. For your specific home, always confirm with the city, county, or agency involved.

**This isn't legal or tax advice.** I'm a real estate agent, not a lawyer or a CPA. This guide explains how things generally work. For your own situation, talk to the right professional.

**Every home is different.** Not everything in this guide will apply to your sale. Most of it only matters if it touches your home or your situation.
